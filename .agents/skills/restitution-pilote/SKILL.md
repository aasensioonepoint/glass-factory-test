# Restitution au pilote — TOUT passe par le communicator, pas seulement les décisions

Moved VERBATIM out of `AGENTS.md` (P4 — the router becomes thin, the rules do
not change). The router keeps a short invariant and points here for the detail.

**Every message the orchestrator addresses to the pilot passes through `communicator` before being displayed — not only decision requests.** This used to cover the four decision gates alone (plan approval, blocked task, class-3 security decision, plan-critic escalation), and that was too narrow by construction: a pilot does not only need help ARBITRATING, he needs help UNDERSTANDING. A raw console is the kit's own jargon — agent names, stage vocabulary, exit codes, `T-NNNNNNNN` and `L-NNNNNNNN` identifiers, SHAs, mutation verdicts — and a pilot who has to decode it is doing the translator's work himself. Demanded explicitly by the pilot on 2026-08-06, after a session where the reports were accurate and unreadable.

What passes through it, i.e. anything that CLOSES a turn toward the pilot: a decision request (unchanged, keeps its fixed block), an end-of-task report, a result or a verdict, a failure and what it costs, a blocker, a refusal, a progress point, an answer to a question about the session's state. The `communicator` therefore carries a third mode, `rapport`, beside `décision` and `état`.

What does NOT pass through it, named so the rule stays applicable rather than aspirational: an agent's own status banner (one line, already bounded), a tool's raw output while work is in flight (that is a trace, not an address to the pilot), and the `FORECAST` / est→actual recap blocks whose shape is fixed elsewhere. **The trigger is no longer the message's NATURE but its RECIPIENT** — a stream the pilot may watch is a trace; a message that says "here is where you stand" is a restitution and it goes through the translator. Verbosity is not the test, and neither is importance.

**Two form reflexes, both paid for by a measured failure.** On a consumer project an explanation written directly by the orchestrator — dense with the vocabulary of the trade (predicate, compiler, invariant, disjunction, non-local) — was simply **not understood**: the pilot answered « je n'ai rien compris » and asked for the translator AND a table, by name. The same material restituted as a **table of cases** was understood and arbitrated in the same breath. So: (a) prefer a TABLE whenever there are CASES to distinguish, and the column shape that works is `situation → ce qui devrait se passer → ce qui se passe`; (b) **BAN the jargon rather than explain it** — a glossed technical term is still a term the pilot must decode, and an explanation that needs a glossary has already failed.

### Le compte rendu se fait sur ÉTAT BALAYÉ, jamais sur événement d'agent
The defect this closes is STRUCTURAL, not a lapse of care, and it was measured on a consumer project. When the report fires at the END of an agent: a long agent produces silence; a grave finding buried in a long report gets summarised as good news; and an answer optimised for the question asked exposes only what happens to intersect that question. None of that is fixed by trying harder — the trigger itself is wrong. Three countermeasures, MECHANICAL rather than promises, and they are duties of the ORCHESTRATOR (the `communicator` is read-only and writes no state):

1. **Any `[Blocker]`/`[High]` finding, any `NO_GO`/`FAIL` not re-dispatched in the same breath, and any drift from the approved plan becomes a BOARD TASK IMMEDIATELY** — before writing the reply, not after. The board is what gets swept; memory is not. A finding that lives only in the conversation is a finding that dies at the next compaction.
2. **A sweep of `tasks.py list` over `blocked` + `running` PRECEDES every reply to the pilot**, and its result is READ, never remembered. Re-reading is the whole point: what you remember is what you noticed, and the defect is precisely what you did not.
3. **Every reply to the pilot ends with a `CE QUI BLOQUE` block derived from that sweep — and an EMPTY block is a valid answer that must still be written.** This is the non-obvious half: silence reads as « rien ne bloque » when all it actually says is « je n'ai pas regardé ». Writing the empty block is what makes the difference between the two visible.

Three rules that make the passage worth its cost — they are what the pilot asked for, and each closes a measured defect. **Never an identifier without its substance**: `T-00000122` tells the pilot nothing and forces him to open the board, so write what the task IS in one clause and put the id in parentheses. **A table as soon as several items share the same fields**, prose when the pilot must follow a chain — the deciding question is whether he has to COMPARE lines, and the explanation never goes inside a cell. **What BLOCKS comes first**, with the three things that make a blocker actionable: what is stopped, what stops it, and what the pilot must decide or do to lift it — and always distinguish "blocked awaiting YOUR decision" from "blocked by a technical defect", because the first asks him something and the second informs him.

The degraded path is unchanged and it is what keeps this rule from ever stalling the pipeline: if `communicator` cannot run, the orchestrator restitutes DIRECTLY, itself, in the communicator's own format, headed by `COVERAGE DEGRADED — communicator indisponible, restitution directe`. Never a silence that reads as a restitution already made.
